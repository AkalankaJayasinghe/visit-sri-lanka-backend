Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your products eco-friendly?

+

Our products are made from natural, organic, and sustainably sourced ingredients. We avoid harmful chemicals, use biodegradable packaging, and ensure our manufacturing processes have minimal environmental impact.

Do you offer international shipping?

+

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping costs and delivery times vary by location. We use carbon-neutral shipping methods whenever possible to minimize our environmental footprint.

What is your return policy?

+

We offer a 30-day return policy for unopened products. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, contact us within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

Are your products cruelty-free?

+

Absolutely! We never test on animals and work only with suppliers who share our commitment to cruelty-free practices. All our products are certified cruelty-free.

How can I track my order?

+

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number to track your package on our website or the carrier's tracking page.

Do you offer bulk discounts?

+

Yes, we offer discounts for bulk orders and wholesale partnerships. Contact our sales team at wholesale@ecolife.com for more information about bulk pricing.
